engine spike measures on our own devices.

## Speech engines
- Apple SpeechTranscriber (iOS/macOS 26+): live partials, all 10 languages (zh_CN/TW/HK, yue listed), OS-provided
  model (one-time download), en WER 2.1%; **no CJK benchmark** → measure.
- sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0 runtime, official Flutter plugin, iOS/macOS/Windows): SenseVoice Small (zh/yue/en/ja/ko,
  228 MB int8, batch, fast), streaming Paraformer (zh/yue/en), Qwen3-ASR 0.6B (Apache-2.0, 30 langs + 22 zh
  dialects, int8 0.7–1 GB, peak 1.7–2.9 GB on Mac; hallucinates on silence → VAD), Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (25 EU
  langs, no CJK, CC BY 4.0, very fast via FluidAudio on Apple).
- WhisperKit / whisper.cpp: mature, all languages, pseudo-streaming, large models.
- Licence flags: SenseVoice/Paraformer/Fun-ASR-Nano labelled both Apache-2.0 and FunASR License v1.1; Moonshine
  Korean non-commercial; Nemotron OpenMDW.
- iOS: models delivered via Background Assets (On-Demand Resources deprecated in iOS 27).

## Cleanup LLM
- Apple Foundation Models (~3B, iOS/macOS 26+, Apple Intelligence devices: iPhone 15 Pro+, M1+): all 10 languages,
  4k context (8k on some OS 27 hardware), guided generation, guardrail false positives → always fall back to
  raw text. Translation better via Apple's Translation framework.
- Small open models: Qwen3.5 0.8B/2B/4B and Qwen3 0.6B/1.7B (Apache-2.0, best CJK in class), Gemma 4 E2B/E4B
  (Apache-2.0). Llama 3.2 / SmolLM: no CJK.
- Runtimes: MLX Swift (fastest on Mac), llama.cpp (Metal/CUDA/Vulkan/CPU), Foundry Local / ONNX Runtime GenAI
  (Windows). Phi Silica: Copilot+ only, no Korean, being replaced Jan 2027.
- Latency (≈150 output tokens): M-series Mac 0.3–1.5 s; iPhone 17 Pro (1.7B) 2.5–3 s; iPhone 15 Pro/16 5–8 s
  (1.7B or Foundation Models), 2–4 s (0.6–0.8B).

## Sales
- App Store: offline utilities sell at $8–25 one-time; universal purchase is a clear message vs VoiceInk.
  Free download + non-consumable unlock + feature-limited free tier beats a $0 trial IAP. Family Sharing on.
- Windows direct: plain Stripe = we are the seller (EU non-Union OSS VAT from the first sale, UK within 30 days);
  merchants of record: Stripe Managed Payments (3.5% + processing; limited seller countries; no licence keys),
  Polar (5% + 50¢; licence keys), Paddle. Lemon Squeezy shuts down 2027-03-31. Offline signed licence (Ed25519)
  fits the privacy promise.
- "Data Not Collected": no analytics/crash SDKs, no account, model downloads from a static host; claims allowed:
  "audio and text never leave your device", "works in Airplane Mode", "no account".
