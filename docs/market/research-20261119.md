# Market research — AI dictation / voice keyboards (2026-11-19)

Source: delegated web research (prices largely from third-party/competitor blogs; conflicting sources noted).
Summary kept for planning; verify before using numbers externally.

Takeaways: leaders are subscriptions (~$144/yr). One-time products avoid cloud cost (on-device, BYO key, or
lifetime alongside a subscription).

## Cost (estimates)
Soniox realtime ≈ $0.12/h; Fun-ASR realtime (intl) ≈ $0.17/h. Heavy user (15 h/month) ≈ $1.8–2.6/month; typical
user < $0.5/month. LLM post-processing with a cheap model likely the same order (unverified).

## Pricing options (undecided — user leans to a low-price paid version first, later maybe Free + Pro)
- A: paid one-time + BYO key ($14.99–19.99): no backend; key setup limits reach.
- B: one-time + consumable credits via own proxy: needs backend/accounts/privacy compliance.
- C: free (BYO key or Apple on-device speech) + Pro one-time ($19.99, launch $14.99); credits later.
Apple Small Business Program: 15% (Japan 10% + 5% payment fee).

## Storefronts
- EU: Trader status declaration required (address/phone/email public) — user action.
- JP/KR/TW/HK/Americas: no extra licensing for a foreign developer.
- China mainland: **never** (user decision 2026-11-19): MIIT app filing, generative-AI compliance filings
  (Apple, from 2026-11-16), CAC; free incumbents.

## Languages (value vs effort)
ja > zh-Hant > ko > de > es > zh-Hans (overseas) > fr > pt-BR > it. Soniox covers all (60 languages); Fun-ASR
covers zh + dialects (Cantonese) — other languages vary by snapshot, verify. Default: Soniox for non-Chinese,
Fun-ASR for Chinese/dialects. User decision: do all 10 in i18n-01.

## Risks
